# What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

The breakthrough was utilising 'code generation is cheap' to experiment with different ideas, and interact with them, before deciding if I should commit to the idea or not. Not being bound by technical capabilities, generation time, and even significant token usage, I felt I was freely able to try different ideas or approaches, integrating them into the site, so I can experience it as a user, a refine where needed. Also, integrating tests into the harness gave me a way to verify the site behaved as intended without spending tokens having Claude rediscover bugs I'd encoutered before.

# What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

This project especially showed the increasing importance of ideas, as code is being generated well, and rapidly, in languages I am not very proficient in myself. As a software developer, I am excited about the opportunity to be able to bring my ideas to life, without technical constraints, however also worried, being scary seeing how rapidly AI can develop functional, complex code, without a human.
